Contact Us

Questions, bug reports, template requests, feedback — it's all welcome.

Email

The fastest way to reach us: hello@printweekly.top

We read everything and usually reply within a few days. When reporting a problem, it helps a lot if you include your browser (e.g., Chrome on Windows) and what you expected vs. what happened.

Template requests

Want a daily planner, a monthly calendar, a meal planner or something stranger? Tell us what you'd print. The most-requested templates get built — for free, like everything else here.

Before you write…

  • Printing problems? Check that your browser's print dialog is set to the same paper size you selected (A4 vs Letter) and that margins are set to “None” or “Default”.
  • Saving a PDF? Use the Print / Save as PDF button and choose “Save as PDF” as the destination — no extra software needed.
  • Looking for a template? Browse all free weekly planner printables first — it may already exist.
