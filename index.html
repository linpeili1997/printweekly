Frequently asked questions

Is this weekly planner printable really free?

Yes. Every template on PrintWeekly is free to customize, print and download. There is no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many copies you print for personal use.

How do I save my weekly planner as a PDF?

Click the Print / Save as PDF button, then choose “Save as PDF” as the destination in your browser's print dialog. The planner exports at the exact paper size you selected.

Should I choose the columns or the vertical layout?

Day columns in landscape orientation let you see the whole week at a glance — great for desks and fridges. The vertical list in portrait orientation gives each day more writing room and fits standard binders better.

Will the planner print correctly on my paper?

Yes. The generator has dedicated A4 and US Letter presets with zero margins, so the sheet fills the page edge-to-edge on any home printer.
