Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026. The short version: the planner generator runs entirely in your browser, and we collect as little as we can get away with.

What we collect

Nothing you type into the planner. The generator runs locally in your browser. Your header text, layout choices and colors never leave your device and are not stored on any server.

Basic, anonymous usage data. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically processes standard technical logs such as IP address (in anonymized form), browser type and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

Cookies and third-party services

We may use the following services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Analytics. We may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Google Analytics 4) to understand aggregate traffic patterns — page views, referrers, countries. This data is not used to identify individuals.
  • Advertising. If advertisements are shown, they may be served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to processing. Since we store almost no personal data, in most cases there is simply nothing to access or delete. For any privacy request, contact us via the contact page.

Children's privacy

The site is safe for general audiences, including students and classrooms, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
