How to use it

  1. Pick paper size and week start day, then click Print / Save as PDF. Print a small stack.
  2. On Sunday (or Monday), write 4–6 habits in the left column — no more than six until tracking becomes automatic.
  3. Each evening, check off what you did. Empty squares are information, not failure.
  4. At week's end, fill the notes & rewards box: one line about what worked, one small reward earned.

FAQ

How many habits should I track per week?

Four to six is the sweet spot. The grid holds ten rows, but tracking fewer habits means each one actually gets your attention — and a completed week feels motivating instead of embarrassing.

Weekly or monthly habit tracker — which is better?

Weekly trackers are better for building new habits: seven days feels achievable, and the weekly review keeps you honest. Monthly grids work well later, for maintaining routines you've already locked in.

Can I write my habits on the sheet after printing?

Yes — the habit rows are intentionally blank lines. Write your own habits at the start of the week, then check them off day by day. Nothing is pre-filled, so the same printable fits any goal.
