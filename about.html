About PrintWeekly

Free printable weekly planners and habit trackers — customizable in your browser, printable at home, no signup required.

Why we exist

PrintWeekly started with a simple frustration: most “free printable planner” sites hide the good templates behind email walls, charge for basic layouts, or hand you a static PDF that fits nobody's week. We think a planner should bend to your life — not the other way around.

So everything on this site is generated live in your browser. Choose your paper size, week start day, layout and color; the sheet updates instantly; you print. That's the whole product, and it's free.

How the site works

The entire generator runs on your device — nothing you configure is sent to a server, no account is created, and nothing is stored. When you print or save as PDF, your browser handles it locally. The site itself is a handful of lightweight pages, so it loads fast even on slow connections.

What you can print

  • Weekly planner — landscape columns or vertical list, with goals box and habit strip
  • Undated weekly planner — reusable any week of any year
  • Weekly habit tracker — a full page for up to 10 habits

Everything is free for personal, family and classroom use. Please don't resell or repackage the templates — but do print as many copies as you like.
