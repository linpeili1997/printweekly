Why one page works

A week that fits on one page gets looked at. This template keeps every commitment on a single sheet you can pin to the wall, stick to the fridge, or lay flat on your desk — no flipping between pages, no app switching. The one-page constraint is also a forcing function: if it doesn't fit, it probably isn't a priority this week.

How to use it

  1. Confirm paper size (A4 or US Letter) and week start day.
  2. Type a header like “Week 37” or leave it blank.
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF and pick your printer — or “Save as PDF” for a digital copy.

FAQ

Are all 7 days really on one page?

Yes. The landscape layout fits all seven day columns — Saturday and Sunday included — on a single sheet, so the whole week is visible at one glance.

How do I get the planner as a PDF?

Click the Print / Save as PDF button and choose “Save as PDF” as the printer destination. You get a clean digital PDF at the exact A4 or US Letter size you selected.

Can I start the week on Monday instead of Sunday?

Yes. Switch the “Week starts on” option in the panel and the day columns reorder instantly — Monday-first for European style planning, Sunday-first for American style.
